# Cryptosystem
A **cryptosystem** is a **set of cryptographic algorithms** that map ciphertexts and plaintexts to each other.<br>

<br>

- **Private-key cryptosystems** use the **same key** for **encryption** and **decryption**:
  - 3DES
  - AES
  - ChaCha20
  - Kuznyechik (GOST 34.12-2015)
  - RC4

- **Public-key cryptosystems** use **different keys** (aka **public key** and **private key** pair) for **encryption** and **decryption**:
  - **Digital signature**
  - Diffie–Hellman **key exchange**
  - Elliptic-curve cryptography
  - RSA encryption
